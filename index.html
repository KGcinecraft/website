What we take off your plate

Your footage deserves more than just an edit. It deserves the right story, pacing, music, colour and emotion.

  • Wedding highlight films

    The story of the wedding day, shaped into a film the couple will want to watch again and again.

  • Wedding teasers

    A short, striking first look, ready to share while the excitement is still fresh.

  • Wedding reels

    Vertical edits for Instagram and other social platforms, in step with the main film.

  • Cinematic wedding films

Questions before you send footage

Is the demo free?

Not permanently. We may provide a 60-second demo edit using your footage so you can judge our work before committing. If you like it and want to proceed, the applicable charges for the demo are payable, and further work is paid according to your package or service.

What should I send for the demo?

A small project is enough: a short set of clips from a wedding you've shot, plus any music or style references you like. Share it as a download link, such as Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer.

Who do you work with?

Photographers, filmmakers and wedding studio owners who want dependable post-production, so they can focus on shooting and running their business.

Which package should I choose?

Start with a demo request. After we've seen your footage and understood what you need, we'll suggest the package that fits.

What if I need something specific?

We handle customised wedding post-production requirements too. Tell us what you need and we'll tell you honestly how we can help.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the package and the size of the project. We agree on a clear delivery date with you before we start.
