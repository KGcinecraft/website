What we do with your footage

  • Highlight films

    The story of the day in a few minutes: the vows, the laughter and the small moments, cut to music that fits.

    3 to 6 minutes

  • Teasers and trailers

    A short first look the couple can share within days of the wedding, while everyone is still talking about it.

    1 to 1.5 minutes

  • Feature and documentary edits

    Ceremony, vows and speeches kept whole and paced like a film, not a recording of an event.

    20 to 40 minutes

  • Colour grading

    Cameras matched, skin tones protected, and one consistent look from the first frame to the last.

    Every film

  • Sound design

    Vows and speeches cleaned up, music mixed underneath, and the atmosphere of the venue brought back into the film.

    Every film

  • Reels and social cuts

    Vertical 9:16 edits for Instagram and YouTube Shorts, made from the same graded footage.

    15 to 60 seconds

How a project runs, from footage to final film

  1. Day 0

    Send the footage

    Share a download link and a short brief: songs, must-have moments and any reference films you like.

  2. Day 1 to 2

    Sync and select

    Cameras and audio are synced, clips organised, and the best moments marked before editing begins.

  3. Day 3 to 7

    First cut

    The story is built with music and pacing, then shared as a private review link.

  4. Your review

    Notes and revisions

    Watch the cut and send timestamped notes. The number of revision rounds is agreed in your quote.

  5. Final polish

    Colour and sound

    Grading, audio cleanup and the final mix happen once the picture is locked.

  6. Delivery

    Final files

    4K or HD masters, a YouTube-ready export, and any social cuts you asked for.

Questions before you send footage

What do you need from me to start?

A download link to the footage (Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer), your song choices if you have them, and a short note on the moments that matter most. A reference film you like helps too.

How long does an edit take?

A first cut of a highlight film typically takes about a week from the day the full footage arrives. Teasers are faster, and long feature edits take longer. You get a clear date in your quote.

How many revisions are included?

Revision rounds are included and the number is stated in your quote. Send timestamped notes on the private review link so changes are quick and precise.

Can you work white-label for my studio?

Yes. Videographers and wedding studios can send footage under their own name, and the finished film is delivered ready to hand to your client.

Which formats do you deliver?

4K and HD masters, a web-optimised export for YouTube or Vimeo, and vertical cuts for Instagram if you ask for them.
